Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

1. What we collect

Tomeoki needs no account. The developer collects nothing: no images, read text, notes, folders, location or usage data. There are no ads and no third-party analytics.

2. Access to Photos

Only screenshots in your photo library are read. Images and their text are handled on your iPhone and never sent anywhere.

3. Text reading and AI titles

Text is read with Apple's Vision framework and AI titles come from Apple Intelligence's on-device model, both on your iPhone.

4. Where data is stored

Folders, notes and read text are stored only on this iPhone. Screenshots moved into the app are also only on this iPhone and are not synced to iCloud.

5. Share sheet

Images saved from the share sheet wait in the app's shared area on your iPhone until the app opens and files them.

6. Notifications

Look Later reminders are created on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere.

7. Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple; the developer never sees your payment details.

8. Deleting your data

Settings > Delete All Data deletes the app's data. Screenshots in the Photos app are not touched.
