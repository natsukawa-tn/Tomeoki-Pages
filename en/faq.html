Help

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Can I get a deleted screenshot back?

Yes. Deleted screenshots stay in Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days. Restore them from there.

New screenshots don't show up

With Limited access, Tomeoki only sees the photos you picked. In the Settings app > Tomeoki > Photos, choose Full Access.

Reading the text takes a while

The first time, Tomeoki reads the text of every screenshot in turn, starting with the ones to sort. You can sort while it works.

The kind is wrong

Open the screenshot > Details > Kind and pick another. Your choice is kept.

How do I save from another app?

Tap Share on an image and choose Tomeoki to save it with a folder and a note. If it isn't listed, add it from More at the end of the share sheet.

Is it free?

Yes. Sorting 30 screenshots a day, text reading, search and up to 3 folders are free. Premium removes the limits on sorting and folders.

What are Cleanup Suggestions?

Tomeoki finds expired coupons, past events, the same screen captured more than once and screenshots left unsorted for 90 days. Deleting them together is a Premium feature.

Screenshots don't show up in iPhone search

Check that Settings > Show in iPhone Search is on. While the app is locked with Face ID, screenshots stay out of search to keep them private.

What is Move into Tomeoki?

A Premium feature that keeps screenshots inside the app and removes them from Photos, so your camera roll holds only photos. They go away if you delete the app, so save the ones you want to keep to Photos.

I don't see AI titles

They need iOS 26 or later on a device with Apple Intelligence turned on (Premium).

What happens when I change iPhones?

Folders and notes are stored only on the iPhone and are not synced to iCloud. Screenshots in Photos move over with iCloud Photos and show up as screenshots to sort on the new iPhone.
