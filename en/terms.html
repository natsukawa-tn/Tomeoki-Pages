Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-10-19

1. The service

Tomeoki is a tool to sort, classify and search the screenshots in your photo library.

2. Deleting screenshots

Screenshots are deleted only when you allow it on iPhone's confirmation screen, and they move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app. Restore them from the Photos app.

3. Images kept in the app

Screenshots moved into the app are stored only on this iPhone and are lost if you delete the app. Save the ones you want to keep to Photos.

4. Accuracy

Text reading and automatic sorting are not perfect; treat the results as a guide.

5. Premium

Premium is bought on the App Store. The yearly plan renews automatically; cancel any time in App Store settings at least 24 hours before renewal.
