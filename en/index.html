Tomeoki

Screenshots, sorted and easy to find

Tomeoki reads the text in your screenshots on your iPhone and sorts them into coupons, places, bookings, recipes and more. Search any word, and use the links, addresses and coupon codes inside.

Swipe to sort

Left to delete, right to keep, tap a folder to file it. Deletions are confirmed once, at the end.

Search the text inside

Find a screenshot by any word in it: a product, a shop, a coupon code, an address.

Sorted by kind

Shopping, places, coupons, receipts, events, recipes, reading and QR codes, automatically.

Your images stay on your iPhone

Reading and sorting happen on the device. No servers of ours, no account.

Tomeoki organizes the screenshots in the Photos app. Deleting a screenshot always goes through iPhone's confirmation and moves it to Recently Deleted.
